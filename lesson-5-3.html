Lesson 5.3 excerpt

Automate only what you have already done by hand.

Claude is useful inside automations when you can recognise a good output before the automation exists. If you cannot describe the standard, Claude cannot reliably hit it.

The mistake is to start with Zapier, Make, or n8n. The better move is to run the task manually with Claude until the patterns are obvious.

The three questions:

  1. Have I done this manually enough times to know the edge cases?
  2. Can I write down what a good output looks like?
  3. Am I willing to review the output after I step out of the loop?
