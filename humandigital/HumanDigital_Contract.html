01 · The work

Statement of Work

1.1 Scope of services

Commercial Growth will deliver the following services to Human Digital over an 8-week engagement from 25 May 2026 to 17 July 2026. This engagement is structured as a working preview of the Head of Growth function with AJ Kavanagh in the seat, with a defined decision point at the conclusion of the term on whether to convert to a permanent Head of Growth role:

  • Build and launch 4 Dripify outbound sequences (1 Master Wide Cast + 3 Niche Lean sequences anchored to existing HumanDigital case studies, sequenced Lean A / B / C across the first month)
  • Configure 4 LinkedIn Sales Navigator saved searches (Core tier) with Boolean filters, Industry taxonomy, and Spotlight intent layers
  • Triage and respond to Human Digital's existing LinkedIn inbox backlog of approximately 420 unread messages
  • Set up HubSpot pipeline reporting cadence and lead-to-deal workflow
  • Provide recommendations for Ben van Rooy's LinkedIn profile rebuild
  • Build analytics dashboard tracking Sales Nav, Dripify, and HubSpot performance
  • Design and specify two AI-powered lead magnets end-to-end (questions, AI prompts, journey map, copy, landing page wireframe, nurture sequence). Build execution to be delivered by HumanDigital's existing development and design team to AJ's specification.
  • Document buying signal maps for each ICP niche lean
  • Deliver complete handover playbook including 90-day continuation plan
  • Facilitate a Head of Growth role conversation in the final week of the engagement, covering performance to date, ongoing scope, and terms of a potential permanent transition

1.2 Responsibilities of the Provider (Commercial Growth)

In addition to the scope above, AJ Kavanagh as Provider will personally:

  • Monitor active Dripify campaigns and respond to all replies in a timely manner during business hours (AEST)
  • Conduct qualified lead conversations arising from the campaigns. Ownership of demonstration calls to be confirmed with Ben van Rooy in Week 1
  • Deliver work products in formats compatible with HumanDigital's existing tools (HubSpot, Dripify, Sales Navigator, LinkedIn)

1.3 Responsibilities of the Client (Human Digital)

  • Provide timely administrative access to Dripify, Sales Navigator, HubSpot, and LinkedIn admin tools by start of Week 1
  • Provide final approval on outbound copy and creative within 48 hours of submission
  • Execute Ben van Rooy's LinkedIn profile rebuild per the recommendations delivered in Week 1
  • Allocate HumanDigital development and design team capacity to execute the lead magnet builds per AJ's specifications across the engagement (lead magnet #1 in Weeks 3-4, lead magnet #2 in Weeks 5-7)
  • Pay invoices in accordance with clause 2.2

03 · General terms

The fine print, in plain English

3.1 Term and termination

This agreement starts on 25 May 2026 and ends on 17 July 2026 unless extended or converted to a permanent engagement by mutual written agreement. The parties agree to a structured Head of Growth role conversation in the final week of the term (commencing 13 July 2026) to assess performance and discuss conversion to a permanent role. Either party may terminate this agreement at any time with seven (7) days written notice. On termination, the Client will pay for all hours worked up to the date of termination.

3.2 Intellectual property

All work products delivered under this agreement (including sequences, copy, documentation, journey maps, lead magnet specifications, and playbooks) become the property of Human Digital upon full payment of the relevant invoice. The Provider retains the right to reference the engagement in case studies and portfolio materials in non-confidential summary form, with prior written approval from the Client.

3.3 Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential all information disclosed during the engagement, including but not limited to client lists, pricing, strategy documents, and operational data. Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this agreement for a period of two (2) years.

3.4 Independent contractor

The Provider performs this engagement as an independent contractor and not as an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of the Client. The Provider is responsible for their own taxes, superannuation, and insurance.

3.5 Liability

The Provider's total liability under this agreement is limited to the total fees paid by the Client under this Statement of Work. The Provider is not liable for any indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

3.6 Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia. Any disputes will be resolved through good-faith negotiation between the parties first, and if unresolved, by mediation before any formal legal action.

3.7 Entire agreement

This Statement of Work, together with the accompanying Proposal document (HumanDigital_Proposal_Page.html, , 19 May 2026), constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. Any amendments must be agreed in writing and signed by both parties.
